# Artwork provenance

`SCUMMVM.png` — original work, authored in this repository by the
`scripts/` icon generator recorded in its commit history. No third-party
asset, photograph, render, or trademark is included.

Released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication), so a developer copying
this repository as a template inherits no attribution obligation from the
example art.

The icon is a generic speech balloon. It deliberately depicts no game, no
character, and no publisher's mark: a content pak that shipped somebody's
box art or logo would put an obligation on every device that installs it.

## If you replace it

- Keep it 256×256 RGBA PNG. That is what the launcher's icon packs use.
- Record where your replacement came from in this file, with its licence.
- Public-domain photography, AI renders, and CC BY-SA material all carry
  different obligations, and they do not all survive redistribution on an SD
  card image. Check before you ship.
